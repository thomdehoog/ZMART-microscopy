changes reload live. Analysis workers stay warm for the bridge's lifetime, so after
installing anything into a `ZMART--*` env, restart the window too.

     store, the checkpoint and the plan agree to 1 µm;
   - in Chromium (walk spec, mock, built page) the shift is **zero** at four zoom depths
     and in every quarter; this is now a permanent check in `walk.spec.js`
     ("masks over nuclei");

   Next: measure inside the operator's window. Open it with
   `WEBVIEW2_ADDITIONAL_BROWSER_ARGUMENTS=--remote-debugging-port=9222`, get masks on

   prints the shift whole and per quarter at four zooms and saves screenshots under

## Contracts settled with the operator today

- The z-stack is the microscope's own: the operator configures it on the job in LAS X;
  the driver presses acquire once and takes what comes back, centred on the stage's z.
- Transparency of a picture is all or nothing per tile.
- Colour/grey, the Z slider and the chips belong to the acquisition on show.
- The gallery pair follows the newest frame until the operator chooses one.
