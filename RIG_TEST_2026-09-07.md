## 7. After the session of 2026-09-07 — what was found, what is open

Fixed on the branch that evening (seven commits, `9fb56cd0..06e9c57a`):
the validators' CI failures, the read after a capture, the stage mark
queuing behind a run and flickering between sites, the overview greying
on step 9, moves that read the stage for nothing, and the first focus
point of a run being lost on an older failure. A target site went from
~31 s to ~8 s plus the move; per-site timing prints as `site n/N timing_s:`.

Still open, in order of value:

- **The log reader hangs.** `run_ci.py --hardware`'s log-reader validator
  leg spun forever on the select-job confirmation and had to be killed;
  in the window the log leg lost most job-selection races. Give
  `readers/patient.py` / the log wait a hard ceiling, or confirm selection
  via the API only.
- **Stacked read retries.** `api_reader` retries 3 × 1 s, the router caps
  it, and `patient.answered` wraps both for 30 s. One layer should do.
- **30 s patience on the status ping** in `get_info`: a ping should answer
  or fail in a second.
- **Windows long paths on the rig.** ~30 offline tests fail under the
  default pytest temp dir (paths > 260 chars). Enable long paths in the
  registry, or run with `--basetemp=C:\tmp\pt` as was done here.
- **Export completion polling** at 0.5 s with three size-stable readings:
  ~1 s per site that could be 0.1 s.
- **The focus job.** The only z-stack job on Y42H93 was `AF Job`, which
  `set_state` refuses on purpose; focus maps that evening ran on whatever
  the recording named. Decide which job is the focussing job and record on it.
- **Validators select `AF Job`** in their job round-trip. It went fine three
  times, but it is the job the rule says never to select programmatically.
